MicroBlaze: gas and ld fixes, CFI directives, PC-relative .eh_frame data

This series is the still-open MicroBlaze binutils work from the
investigation that produced the relaxation fix already in master
(a639e0adaf2, f55053f1dc9).  It applies to current master with git am in
this order.  Patches 1-3 are independent bug fixes; 4 and 5 add what a
PC-relative .eh_frame needs (5 depends on 4: both touch tc-microblaze.h
and the cfi test); 6 and 7 are testsuite-only.

  1/7  microblaze: write the value for BFD_RELOC_8 and BFD_RELOC_16 fixups
       md_apply_fix had no case for them, so ".dc.b L1-L0" assembled to 0
       and no relocation survived to correct it.
  2/7  bfd: microblaze: keep the PLT address of address-taken functions
       finish_dynamic_symbol zeroed st_value unconditionally, so &func in
       a non-PIC executable differed from &func inside the library.
  3/7  bfd: microblaze: apply relocations the .eh_frame editor asks to keep
       _bfd_elf_section_offset returning -2 was treated as "skip" instead
       of "apply statically", leaving the field unrelocated.
  4/7  gas: Support CFI directives for MicroBlaze
       TARGET_USE_CFIPOP was never defined; gas rejected .cfi_* with "CFI
       is not supported for this target".  Adds the hooks, register-name
       mapping, and a testsuite.
  5/7  microblaze: support "sym - ." across sections, and relax
       R_MICROBLAZE_32_PCREL
       DIFF_EXPR_OK was never defined, so gas could not assemble the
       difference a PC-relative .eh_frame pointer is made of.
  6/7  ld/testsuite: widen the pr24511 xfail to all MicroBlaze targets
       elfmicroblaze.sc defines no __init_array_start, so the test fails
       rather than xfails on every non-elf MicroBlaze triple.
  7/7  gas: testsuite: exclude microblazeel from the diff1 test
       The guard matched microblaze-* but not microblazeel-*.

Testing: with the series on master, the MicroBlaze gas suite
(gas/microblaze/*.exp) and ld-microblaze plus ld-elf/eh* pass with no
failures, and the whole gas, ld and binutils suites for microblaze-elf show
no change other than the tests the series adds.  The set was also used
end to end: with 4/7 and 5/7 the gcc MicroBlaze port can switch its
.eh_frame encoding to PC-relative (patch to follow on gcc-patches), which
took libc.so from 711 dynamic relocations in .eh_frame to none and made
the section read-only with an .eh_frame_hdr table; glibc's full make check
under qemu showed no unwinding or exception-table regression.
